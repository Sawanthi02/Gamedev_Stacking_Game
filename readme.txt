Sawanthi Mooijman 22158480 gold 2 stacking game

minecraft inspired stacking game. goal is to stack the items as high as possible without any falling off the crafting table.
each item has its own unique traits:
	
	Stick -> most basic item of the game with a small bounce. the long and flat shape makes it a life save in tricky situations
	Iron Ingot -> small but heavy object that has great impact when landing
	Slime Ball -> round and bouncy. extremely bouncy when landing on another slime ball
	Honey Block -> big and sticky. objects won't bounce off. perfect against slime balls
	Feather -> light and low gravity. easy to place, but easy to get moved around as well
	Totem -> oddly shaped but won't kill you when falling off.

move the mouse to move the object, leftclick to place.
